CareerBridge Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

CareerBridge is a career support application published by Anfaal's Tech. It provides job-search tools, saved jobs, CV-building features, career resources, scholarship information, and AI-assisted career guidance.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on how you use the app, information you enter may include CV details, education, skills, work experience, career preferences, saved-job choices, and messages sent to the AI assistant. Please avoid entering information you do not want processed by the relevant service.

2. AI Assistant and Third-Party Services

When you use the AI career assistant, your message is sent through a CareerBridge server hosted by Cloudflare to Google Gemini to generate a response. The message and related request information may be processed by these providers to deliver the service.

Google's free-tier AI service may use prompts and responses to improve its services, and human reviewers may review them, subject to Google's applicable terms and policies. Do not submit passwords, financial account details, government identification numbers, confidential employment information, or other sensitive personal information to the AI assistant.

AI-generated information may be inaccurate or incomplete. Verify important career, employment, education, and financial decisions independently.

3. Job and Scholarship Information

The app may display job listings, scholarship information, and external resources. When you open a third-party website or service, that provider's own privacy policy and practices apply.

4. Storage and Retention

Some app information, such as saved jobs or CV details, may be stored locally on your device, depending on the feature. Information sent to third-party services may be handled and retained according to their policies and technical requirements. This policy does not promise that all information is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app.

5. Sharing of Information

CareerBridge does not sell your personal information. Information may be transmitted to service providers when necessary to operate a feature, including Cloudflare and Google Gemini for AI responses. Information may also be disclosed where required by law. The exact data handled depends on the features you use.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to support the operation and security of the service. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Avoid submitting sensitive or confidential information.

7. Children's Privacy

CareerBridge is a career-support application and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the service, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

8. Your Choices

You can choose what information to enter into the app and whether to use the AI assistant. You can stop using a feature at any time. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored information, but it does not necessarily delete information already processed by third-party providers.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised when appropriate.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the CareerBridge developer through the contact details provided on the app's Google Play listing.
